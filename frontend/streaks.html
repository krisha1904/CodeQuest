📜 CodeQuest Daily Streak Rules
🎯 Qualifying Learning Activities Only completing a Java lesson or passing a topic quiz with ≥ 70% counts toward your streak. Merely logging in does not count as learning activity.
📅 One Credit Per Calendar Day Multiple lessons completed on the same day count as 1 streak day. Consistency beats cramming!
⏰ User Timezone Calculation Streaks reset according to your configured local timezone (defaults to Asia/Kolkata).
🔄 Streak Reset Mechanism Missing a calendar day drops your active streak back to 0. Resume learning on any day to start a fresh consecutive run.
